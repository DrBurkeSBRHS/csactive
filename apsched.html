Content Prioritization & Pacing Rules

Python programming fundamentals (Lessons 1.1–1.2), Internet protocols & encryption (Lessons 2.1–2.2), Data processing & visualization (Lessons 3.1–3.2), and the 12-hour Create PT studio block.
